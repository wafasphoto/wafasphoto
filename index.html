ABOUT THE PHOTOGRAPHER

Images that feel
like a memory.

Wafas Photo is a visual photography practice by Fikri Wafa, based in Samarinda. The work focuses on honest moments, clean composition and color with a cinematic editorial feel.

From graduation portraits and intimate engagements to events and visual stories for local brands, every frame is made to feel personal, intentional and timeless.
